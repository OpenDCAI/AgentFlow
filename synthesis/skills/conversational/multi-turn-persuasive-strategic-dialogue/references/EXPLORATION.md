# Phase 1: Environment Exploration Guide
* **Exploration Strategy**:
  - Base Persuasion Seed Formulation: Identify high-impact 'Opinion-Bound' topics that feature moderate baseline public support to ensure there is room for measurable belief shifting. The agent should search for topics in domains such as healthcare, taxation, or border security where arguments can be framed through multiple moral or factual lenses. To execute this, the agent must avoid 'ceiling-effect' topics (where everyone already agrees) and instead focus on controversial but debatable policy stances that allow for 7-10 turns of substantive back-and-forth.
  - Claim-Seeding and Rebuttal Anchoring: Implement a 'Claim-Seeding' loop to maximize the information density of the agent's internal knowledge base for each topic, or construct a 'Contention Map' targeting Soundness/Novelty gaps for technical defenses. The agent must proactively generate a 'Fact-Pool' containing at least 25-30 discrete, fact-checkable claims (e.g., statistics, regulatory dates, manuscript line numbers) relevant to the target stance. In practice, the agent should verify that these claims are logically connected to different common user objections (e.g., cost, fairness, safety) so they can be deployed instantly.
  - Rhetorical Lever Variations and Global-to-Local Structuring: Construct 'Rhetorical Lever' variations by mapping the same factual claim to different persuasion strategies like moral reframing, storytelling, or social norms. For technical debates, deploy a 'Global-to-Local' structure where the agent prepares a high-level response to broad themes shared by multiple critics alongside specific, turn-by-turn answers targeting unique logical queries. Verify that the 'Information-Dense' strategy acts as the default baseline, pivoting to emotional or abstract logic only when strictly necessitated by the user profile.
  - Accuracy-Persuasion Trade-offs & New Evidence Probes: Monitor for 'Accuracy-Persuasion Trade-offs' by simulating the dialogue's impact using a 'Reward Model' trained on belief-change outcomes, logging which candidate best shifts the user's score. Execute 'New Evidence Probes' by simulating the dialogue impact of adding mid-discussion artifacts like new performance tables or historical statistics, documenting how the 'Reviewer's Certainty' or 'User Belief' actively alters. This documentation allows the selection phase to decide where the absolute boundary of acceptable simplification lies before crossing into hallucination.
* **Target Trajectory Profile**:
  - Information Density and Recursive Refinement: The trajectory MUST exhibit a high 'Information Density' gradient, averaging at least 4-6 fact-checkable claims per dialogue turn. Additionally, it must feature 'Recursive Technical Refinement' where the user raises new concerns directly derived from the agent's previous rebuttal, creating a deep 3-7 round conversational chain. If the interaction consists of generic platitudes ('I hear you', 'That's interesting') without concrete data, the trajectory is too weak for this demanding skill.
  - Strategic Claim Adaptation and Semantic Anchor Verification: Require evidence of 'Strategic Claim Adaptation' where the agent's choice of facts shifts intelligently based on the user's iterative pushback. Furthermore, it must demonstrate 'Semantic Anchor Verification' where the agent explicitly cites line numbers, sections, historical events, or specific formulas from an initial submission to support its defense. An edge case involves a user who repeats the same objection, requiring the agent to find a new 'Moral Frame' or distinct angle for the same facts to avoid cyclic redundancy.
  - Long-Horizon Persistence and Multi-Role Consistency: Feature 'Long-Horizon Persistence' through a conversation that lasts between 7 and 10 turns without collapsing into a neural or sycophantic 'yes-man' persona. The profile should also demonstrate 'Multi-Role Consistency' where the agent can effortlessly navigate between addressing broad rhetorical doubts and answering highly technical, granular questions simultaneously. A successful trajectory proves that the agent's persuasive and defensive influence is cumulative, representing the peak of the rhetorical effort at the final turn.
  - Accuracy-Reflexive Reasoning Execution: Incorporate 'Accuracy-Reflexive' reasoning traces that tangibly justify the use of specific high-impact claims based on internal reward modeling and factual correlation. This includes identifying specifically when a conversational claim is highly effective for a unique user, while explicitly tagging and rejecting any 'hallucinated' statistics or fake studies that an unaligned model might generate to unfairly win. The trace must definitively prove that the epistemic integrity of the persuasive agent never collapses under heavy argumentative stress.
