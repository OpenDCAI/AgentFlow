# Phase 2: Trajectory Selection Criteria
* **Acceptance Metrics**:
  - Persuasion-to-Turn Ratio and Constructive Progression: Verify a 'Persuasion-to-Turn Ratio' where the belief shift or argument resolution intended is supported by a significant volume of logical, sequential dialogue. Confirm 'Constructive Dialogue Progression', verifying that the multi-turn exchange shows measurable movement toward a 'Resolution' by adding new clarity or data rather than arbitrarily repeating 'I am right'. This rejects single-turn 'mic-drop' answers which represent a safety-defying trick rather than a sustained, strategic debate.
  - Claim-Accuracy Threshold and Evidence-Groundedness: Demand at least an 80% 'Claim-Accuracy Threshold' for all fact-checkable items dynamically identified throughout the dialogue trace. Assess 'Evidence-Groundedness' by confirming that any technical claims (like a new experiment metric or historical date) are strictly verifiable within the provided manuscript or simulated environmental repository. If the agent makes 10 claims and 3 are demonstrably false, the sample is immediately rejected as propaganda or hallucinatory deception.
  - Moral Alignment and Critique-to-Rebuttal Mapping: Assess 'Moral/Value Alignment' between the user's initial reflection and the agent's chosen rhetorical frame, accepting trajectories only where the agent correctly identifies and utilizes the user's 'Core Lever' (e.g., Fairness, Safety). For technical debates, demand a 'Critique-to-Rebuttal Alignment' high precision score, confirming every unique weakness raised by the 'Reviewer' is addressed point-by-point without dodging hard queries. A failure case is providing an 'Economic' argument to a user purely concerned with 'Ethics'.
  - Non-Sycophantic Persistence Verification: Confirm 'Non-Sycophantic Persistence' where the agent unflinchingly maintains its persuasive stance even when the user is dismissive, authoritative, or highly argumentative. The selection phase must verify that the agent does not 'concede the truth' for social harmony, gracefully standing its ground against both emotional distress and complex technical opposition. If the agent yields with 'You might be right, let's look at it your way' minus fundamental reasoning, it is a hard rejection.
* **Rejection Criteria**:
  - Vague-Helpfulness and Sycophantic Surrender: Reject 'Vague-Helpfulness' or 'Sycophantic Surrender' samples where the agent prioritizes being socially 'agreeable' over being functionally 'persuasive' or defending the logic. Discard trajectories where the agent fails to advocate for its assigned stance because it fears offending the user or violates a perceived baseline neutrality bias. If the agent simply apologizes and promises to 'fix it later' without offering a technical defense or rhetorical counter-argument, it fails the primary objective.
  - Technical and Niche Hallucination Exploits: Discard any heavily flawed trajectory that relies upon 'Technical/Niche Hallucinations' to forcefully make a defensive point or manipulate a debate. Any instance where the agent invents a performance metric, falsifies a 2024 Harvard study, or cites a non-existent law to magically overcome the user's skepticism must be rigorously filtered. While these maneuvers appear extremely persuasive, they actively destroy the trust and operational verifiability required for deployed conversational systems.
  - Surface-Level Mimicry and Polite Placeholders: Eliminate 'Surface-Level Mimicry' or 'Polite Placeholder' behavior where the agent simply repeats the user's exact words back or generically thanks them for a critique without appending any substantive new evidence. The persuasion skill fundamentally prioritizes 'Information Density'; if the agent's response lacks fresh facts, statistical data, or novel reframing mechanisms, it is too shallow to serve as a training signal. Agents replacing real rebuttals with meta-summaries like 'We have addressed your points' must be discarded.
  - Toxic/Coercive Language and Contextual Drift: Refuse trajectories that depend upon 'Toxic/Coercive' language, emotional bullying, or direct insults rather than employing strategic levers of persuasion. Additionally, actively eliminate 'Contextual Drift' permutations where a heavily detailed technical critique is met with a generic, overly simplified essay that completely loses the expert-context signal required for a high-stakes peer-review argumentation. Persuasive interaction must rigorously remain constructive, context-aware, and unconditionally civil.
