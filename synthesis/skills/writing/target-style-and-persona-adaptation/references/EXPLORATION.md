# Phase 1: Environment Exploration Guide
* **Exploration Strategy**:
  - Conduct a 'Stylometric Fingerprint Extraction' to identify the target's underlying linguistic patterns for both syntax and vocabulary. Look for unconscious markers like the frequency of prepositional phrases, preferred dialogue verbs, or implicit structural beats standard to the requested domain.
  - Perform a 'Subjective Lexical Audit' if the target persona is objective/encyclopedic. Identify evaluative modifiers, superlative adjectives, and editorializing adverbs (e.g., 'interestingly', 'exceptional') that impose a value judgment, separating 'Contested Assertions' from 'Established Facts'.
  - Perform a 'Stereotypical Spillover and Bias Audit' to sever unintended associations between the target role and generic AI behaviors. Blacklist standard 'AI-assistant' transition phrases (e.g., 'In conclusion,' 'I hope this helps') to ensure the resulting text remains 'in-character'.
  - Implement 'Domain-Specific Collocation and Authority Mapping' to lock in the required word pairings that immediately signal deep industry competence. Consult internal knowledge to locate canonical anchor phrases (e.g., 'in accordance with' for legal texts, 'statistically significant' for academia).
* **Target Trajectory Profile**:
  - The trajectory must record an 'Identity-to-Syntax Map' or 'Genre-to-Structure Map' that plots the intended syntactic histograms or macro-structural beats before generation.
  - A good trajectory documents the 'Minimal Trigger Rationale' identifying the specific author tags, seed words, or legal collocations that will ground the generation.
  - A good trajectory demonstrates 'Fidelity-First Neutralization' if adopting an NPOV persona, ensuring no facts are lost during the bias-removal pass, modifying only the subjective framing markers.
  - The trajectory prioritizes 'Semantic Fidelity' over 'Surface-Level Complexity' when upscaling phrasing for professional genres, avoiding pseudo-professional 'archaic slop' in favor of precise technical lexicons.
