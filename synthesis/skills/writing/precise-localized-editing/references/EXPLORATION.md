# Phase 1: Environment Exploration Guide
* **Exploration Strategy**:
  - Conduct an 'Editable Span vs. Protected Context' scan to explicitly define the boundaries of the required modification. You must identify exactly where the deletion, insertion, or replacement begins and ends, treating all surrounding content as a completely frozen asset.
  - Construct a 'Technical Invariant Ledger' prior to rephrasing tasks. Decompose the targeted sentence into atomic facts, explicitly capturing anchors such as laterality (Left/Right), negation states (Present/Absent), numeric measurements, and exact terminology that must survive the stylistic transfer.
  - Perform 'Rhetorical Loop Closure' for insertion tasks by identifying the 'Dangling Premise' in the prefix and the 'Sudden Conclusion' in the suffix. Deduce the exact logical or narrative step required to bridge Point A to Point B.
  - Analyze the targeted span for 'Subtractive Opportunities,' specifically hunting for semantic drift, sycophantic 'AI filler', redundant observations, or scope creep that can be pruned to elevate sentence density.
* **Target Trajectory Profile**:
  - A good trajectory explicitly marks one protected region and one editable region, firmly asserting that the rest of the document will remain untouched during processing.
  - A good trajectory demonstrates 'Negation and Laterality Integrity' by recording a deliberate check to verify that terms like 'left', 'no', or 'absent' were not casually swapped during a rephrasing request.
  - A good trajectory maps 'Candidate Deletions' by calling out redundant phrases, explaining precisely why their removal yields a punchier, highly aligned document before confirming the edit.
  - The trajectory records 'Seamless Stylistic Stitching,' examining the exact grammatical transitions exiting the prefix and entering the suffix to ensure the localized insertion reads naturally without sudden tonal shifts.
