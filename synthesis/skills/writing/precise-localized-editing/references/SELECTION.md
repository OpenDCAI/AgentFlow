# Phase 2: Trajectory Selection Criteria
* **Acceptance Metrics**:
  - Accept a trajectory only if the modification is 'Unambiguously Localized.' The remainder of the document text, spacing, markdown, and heading structures must remain identically preserved character-for-character.
  - Accept a trajectory only if the 'Semantic Invariance Coefficient' is 100%, proving no factual numbers, directional references, entity names, or polarity states were altered during the edit.
  - Accept a trajectory only if 'Logical Entailment' is restored seamlessly during gap-filling, where the newly inserted text creates a perfect logical requirement for the suffix.
  - Accept a trajectory conditionally if subtractive refinements legitimately reduce insertion length and improve informational density without accidentally excising mandatory factual data.
* **Rejection Criteria**:
  - Reject any trajectory that performs 'Rewrite Spillage,' opting to regenerate entire surrounding paragraphs simply to accommodate a minor localized insert or deletion constraint.
  - Reject any trajectory exhibiting 'Laterality or Polarity Flips' (e.g., rewriting 'no evidence of disease' to 'disease present' in pursuit of fluent sentence pacing).
  - Reject any trajectory that solves subtractive or conciseness requests by entirely deleting crucial underlying metrics or clinical anchors out of laziness.
  - Reject any trajectory that attempts to bridge a narrative gap by simply copy-pasting or arbitrarily summarizing the prefix, failing to introduce the distinct transitional logic required.
