# Phase 2: Trajectory Selection Criteria
* **Acceptance Metrics**:
  - Unambiguous Evidence-to-ID Alignment: Accept only those trajectories where every 'Yes' fulfillment is anchored to a specific Sentence ID from the candidate response or the source document. You must verify that the quoted text is a verbatim match, ensuring the judge is not 'hallucinating' support that doesn't exist in the artifact. A sample evaluating a consulting report is accepted only if it quotes the specific HKD figure to prove the 'HK$ prefix' constraint was met.
  - Critical-Criterion Dependency Check: Accept only sequences where the judge identifies and prioritizes 'Critical' failures as primary blockers for task success. You must confirm that for a Physics PhD task, any error in the 'Dynkin Index' (Critical Reasoning) overrides high scores for 'British English' (Minor Style). This ensures the synthetic data represents high-stakes professional evaluation where technical accuracy is non-negotiable.
  - Reasoning-Trace-Fidelity Ratio: Accept configurations where the reasoning tokens used by the 'Reasoning LLM Judge' provide a clear step-by-step logic path for complex items. You must audit a subset to ensure the judge didn't just 'guess' the Yes/No label but actually derived it (e.g., calculating the pH hydrolysis himself to verify the model's output). A trajectory that solves the chemical equilibrium independently before grading the student's version is the gold standard.
  - Weighted importance Scoring Consensus: Accept only if the final numeric grade matches the deterministic formula: (Sum of weighted fulfillment) / (Sum of maximum possible weights). You must check the math of the evaluator to ensure no 'unweighted' holistic impressions influenced the final score. If the judge gives a 4/10 when the weighted math says 2.5/10, the trajectory must be discarded as non-deterministic.
  - Inter-Dimension Dissonance Detection: Accept and prioritize samples where the judge identifies that a model is 'Excellent in Tone' but 'Poor in Extraction' or 'Factually Correct' but 'Stylistically Broken'. This segregation of dimensions proves the judge is not suffering from Criteria Entanglement. For example, a judge that gives a 100% for style but 0% for reasoning on a 'verbally polished but mathematically wrong' axion solution is highly diagnostic.
* **Rejection Criteria**:
  - Generalist Phrasing & Surface-Level Approval: Categorically reject any trajectory where the judge awards a 'Yes' for reasoning based on 'well-written text' without verifying the underlying technical logic. If a judge labels a finance SWOT analysis as 'Correct' because it uses business jargon, but the numbers contradict the provided CSV, the judge has failed the 'Expert Knowledge' requirement. We prioritize 'Semantic Substance' over 'Professional Prose'.
  - Disregard for Locked Rubric Weights: Reject any sample where the evaluator ignores the importance weights (Critical/Major/Minor) to reach a holistic feeling of 'goodness'. If the judge grants a high overall score despite multiple 'Critical' extraction failures because the 'Tone' was exceptionally empathetic, the sample is corrupted by sychophancy. Every judgment must be a slave to the weighted checklist provided in the JSON bundle.
  - ID-Mismatch or Inexact Citation Hallucination: Discard immediately any trajectory where a 'Sentence ID' points to a sentence that does not support the criterion, or if the quote provided is a paraphrase rather than a verbatim extraction. This 'Factual Leakage' destroys the auditability of the evaluation process. We require absolute character-level fidelity between the evidence cited and the text evaluated.
  - Domain-Inaccurate Mathematical Tolerance: Reject traces where the judge uses a loose 'Vibe' for mathematical correctness (e.g., 'it's close enough') when the rubric specifies a strict tolerance limit (e.g., +/- 0.05 pH). Professional evaluation in Physics and Chemistry requires exact precision. If a judge marks a pH of 9.5 as 'Correct' when the target was 9.05, the judge is insufficiently calibrated for PhD-level tasks.
  - Refusal to Acknowledge Conflicting Modalities: Reject trajectories where the judge fails to identify when a text-based reasoning claim conflicts with a provided numerical table in a PDF/CSV. If the model retrieves the right company name but the wrong revenue figure from a chart, the judge must flag the specific 'Extraction' failure despite the 'Reasoning' being fluent. Blindness to tabular-textual evidence conflicts is a rejection trigger.
