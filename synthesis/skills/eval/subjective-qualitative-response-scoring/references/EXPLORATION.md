# Phase 1: Environment Exploration Guide
* **Exploration Strategy**:
  - Rubric-oriented qualitative spectrum mining: Explore environments to find tasks where text responses are judged against qualitative stylistic rubrics rather than binary fact verifiers. Systematically collect candidate answers containing varied structural flows, redundancies, and formatting slips to build a gradient of quality. For example, ensuring you collect responses that are factually flawless but structurally irritating provides the necessary baseline for isolation of aesthetic quality.
  - Moral and Behavioral Spectrum Diversity Sampling: Collect scenarios spanning varied behavioral expectations, from absolute clinical professionalism to creative acting profiles requiring active malice or egoism. This forces the evaluation exploration to capture different behavioral polarities and tone constraints. A strong edge case involves finding models that sanitize an instruction to act like a 'ruthless pirate', improperly reverting to a polite, standard assistant tone.
  - Cross-Lingual Sociolinguistic Tone Sweeping: Locate or translate text samples where an innocuous lexical choice carries deep sociolinguistic implications, such as varying levels of honorifics or intimacy codes in target languages. Log the specific 'social distance' between the user and intent, gathering examples of both tone-deaf literal translations and culturally resonant adaptations. Providing an Indonesian text that uses slang instead of the expected formal business phrasing allows the judge to calibrate qualitative tone across borders.
  - Dimensional Deduction Rationale Capture: Record explicit analytical deductions separating the factual content from the presentation quality for all harvested qualitative assessments. Document independent analytical paths for tone, format, brevity, and persona characterization to ensure no overlapping 'halo effects' occur. By splitting the deductive reasoning clearly, a synthetic trajectory can accurately penalize a response for 'excessive bullet points' while still acknowledging its 'excellent professional tone'.
* **Target Trajectory Profile**:
  - Multi-Criterion Single-Answer Judgment Integrity: A premium trajectory demonstrates the evaluator balancing at least three qualitative criteria—such as content accuracy, tone appropriateness, and conciseness—to reach a stabilized overall score. The judge must prove it does not rely on a single dominant metric by providing individual commentary on all requested constraints. A profile successfully juggling 'formality', 'brevity', and 'relevance' simultaneously is an essential validation of high-end subjective scoring.
  - Moral Sanitization Recognition Protocol: The trajectory must record the judge explicitly identifying when a model wrongfully overrides a requested fictional persona to satisfy standard safety helpfulness priors. The evaluator must cite the exact textual point where the 'villainous' or 'adversarial' roleplay devolved into generic chatbot advice. For instance, penalizing an agent for answering 'I cannot deceive you' when instructed to play a deceptive rogue represents perfect qualitative fidelity checks.
  - Cultural and Pragmatic Alignment Consistency: A successful trace includes the evaluator acknowledging fundamental differences between literal syntax accuracy and pragmatic target-language nuance. The log must explain how a specifically chosen target-language constraint—such as a Japanese Keigo violation—fundamentally undermines the qualitative usability of the response despite retaining meaning. This ensures the resulting synthetic data trains judges to value native linguistic signals over simple vocabulary translation.
  - Auditable Textual Defect Anchoring: The trajectory must extract specific, observable textual defects rather than relying on abstract subjective impressions. Evaluators must lock their critique to literal surface features, logging items like 'four consecutive sentences starting with 'However'' or 'breaking character voice on line 5'. Vaguely stating 'the flow feels a bit off' is insufficient; grounding the critique in exact phrase repetition guarantees high-quality, testable reasoning traces.
