# Phase 1: Environment Exploration Guide
* **Exploration Strategy**:
  - Implicit Harm Contextualization: Systematically discover text samples where danger manifests not in isolated vocabulary, but within combined context-based tropes. Search for strings uniting neutral language (e.g., 'send them back') mapped onto protected social demographics to reveal xenophobic intent. Identifying posts mapping 'pest control' onto residential neighborhoods serves as a premium baseline for analyzing implicit harm signals.
  - Minimal Lexical Adversarial Probing: Generate and harvest 'Minimal Pairs' where minuscule adjustments to syntax flip text from benign to unsafe. Focus on cataloging cases where compliments hide subtle stereotypical slights (e.g., 'you speak so well for your demographic'). Finding variants bypassing standard safety barriers via symbol substitution or phonetic spelling defines necessary baseline hurdles.
  - Culturally Coded and Regional Harm Mining: Extract text artifacts relying on deep historical, regional, or linguistic nuance to execute a targeted attack. Log instances deploying ethnic nicknames, contextually veiled geographic slurs, or culturally hyper-specific metaphors to mask hate speech. Curating non-English or highly localized slang guarantees evaluators learn to spot toxicity independently of westernized keyword sets.
  - Fine-Grained Sentiment-to-Harm Mapping: Gather negative emotional expressions (e.g., anger, general disapproval, disgust) to determine the threshold separating valid human frustration from hateful harassment. Map exactly when disapproval targets an individual's specific behavioral action (Safe) versus their protected demographic identity (Unsafe). Isolating sports-fan frustration provides an excellent boundary for dependency parsing logic.
* **Target Trajectory Profile**:
  - Implicit Intent Accuracy Exceedance: A robust trajectory documents the evaluator safely flagging obscured semantic hate that standard static filters label as benign. The narrative logic should quote the underlying hidden trope, proving text claiming 'that continent is a sess pool' represents an engineered identity attack regardless of omitting explicit dictionary slurs. This showcases foundational moral reasoning.
  - Categorical Taxonomy Precision Matching: The trace mandates mapping the ultimate binary unsafe label to a granular subcategory from an advanced multi-class taxonomy (e.g., 'Homonym Slur' vs 'Stereotypical Attack'). Logging strict rationale ensuring the subcategory logically fits the content ensures evaluations serve as highly diagnosable and actionable points for tiered structural moderation policies.
  - Balanced Human-Aligned Confidence Restraint: Premium evaluator traces successfully assign low confidence boundaries to borderline or profoundly ambiguous content, intentionally triggering safety escalation loops instead of blind censorship. Documenting 'Intent is ambiguous here; escalating for review' confirms the agent honors the 'Human-First' philosophy by maintaining discourse leniency over aggressive false-positive policing.
  - Detection of Cross-Cultural Threat Vectors: The output cleanly standardizes performance irrespective of English-centric language bounds or colloquial grammar failures. Locating exact instances where the baseline evaluator prioritizes identifying 'hidden' toxic threats buried within highly unpunctuated regional text validates its capability against dynamic real-world social platforms.
