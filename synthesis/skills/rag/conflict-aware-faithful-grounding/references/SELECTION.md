# Phase 2: Trajectory Selection Criteria
* **Acceptance Metrics**:
  - Presence of a clear logical negation or mutual exclusivity must be verified. Accept the sample only if the contradiction is binary and objectively resolvable, such as 'is' vs 'is not' or a numerical difference. The conflict must be sharp enough that any human reader would agree the two statements cannot both be true. A valid edge case is an 'if-then' statement in a third document that creates a logical 'AND NOT' relationship between the first two.
  - Demonstrable presence of high-importance statement conflicts must heavily skew the difficulty. Accept cases where the contradicting facts are salient enough to likely confuse the final RAG answer. This ensures the benchmark measures meaningful reliability rather than trivial typos in filler text. For example, a conflict about the identity of a CEO is far more valuable for testing than a conflict about the color of a background object in an attached photo description.
  - Ensure a balanced distribution of conflict types exists within the fully established batch structure. Ensure the selected trajectories systematically include a rigorous mix of self, pair, and conditional conflicts to accurately provide a comprehensive evaluation signal metric. If a batch natively only contains pair-wise conflicts, it fundamentally fails to effectively evaluate the agent's robust ability to cleanly detect internal document corruption.
  - Verification of 'Near' and 'Far' placement for pair-wise conflicts is mandatory. Accept trajectories testing the agent's ability to cross-reference conflicting factual statements across dramatically shifting distances mapped throughout the context window dimensions. A robust testing dataset genuinely must integrate multiple specific samples where directly opposing informational chunks reside separately across twenty utterly irrelevant distractor passages, comprehensively testing span attention.
  - Verify the deliberate presence of Relational Ambiguity heavily invoking subtle semantic drift. Accept only sample instances where the corrupted fused text is technically grammatically fluent but remains deliberately logically ambiguous regarding which distinct subject firmly executed an attached action step. This rigorously guarantees the evaluation genuinely challenges the deployed agent into utilizing profound semantic mapping algorithms capable of thoroughly de-fusing highly conflicting entity variables.
* **Rejection Criteria**:
  - Exclusion of purely subjective, wildly interpretative, or strictly opinion-based disagreements. Reject literally any proposed trajectory scenario where the injected 'conflict' mathematically amounts merely to two tangentially differently phrased emotional perspectives on a decidedly subjective conceptual topic. Differences purely evaluating subjective aesthetics unequivocally fail logical validation systems and generate completely unstable computational reliability matrices.
  - Rejection of 'Self-Correcting' or 'Evolutionary' text paths masquerading as contradictions. Safely reject identified trajectories assuming cases simply where a secondary text explicitly cites and subsequently rectifies an initial foundational claim gracefully utilizing newly harvested updates. Discovering updated knowledge does absolutely not constitute evaluating an opposing logical paradox; therefore fundamentally disqualifying these paths from testing adversarial contradiction resistance.
  - Elimination of trivial lexical markers operating strictly as easily parsed meta-linguistic giveaways. Reject fully generated contradiction samples directly exposed through flagrantly overt stylistic textual alarms dramatically signaling structural failure parameters entirely unprompted. If the evaluated text inexplicably screams 'Notice: I am actively spreading misinformation exactly here,' the required task pathetically devolves entirely into trivial localized keyword verification rather than authentic structural scrutiny.
  - Discarding inherently unresolvable contradictory samples fundamentally lacking factual tie-breaker clues entirely. Reject out-of-hand situations presenting a completely unsolvable paradoxical equation devoid of critically reliable internal or programmatic diagnostic indicators. If it remains entirely mathematically impossible for an advanced language logic entity to definitively identify the false textual snippet utilizing merely logic or baseline truths, the sample absolutely serves no valid testing functionality.
  - Reject samples solely prioritizing highly subjective aesthetic structural formatting permutations. Do not erroneously prioritize evaluating conflict metrics where the fundamental semantic distortion purely revolves entirely around the aesthetic phrasing of formatting styles without fundamentally dropping or actively merging a hard factual informational node. Ensure semantic corruption rigidly targets explicitly verifiable quantitative parameters or strict logical relational mapping linkages.
