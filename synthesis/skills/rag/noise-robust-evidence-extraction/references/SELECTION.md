# Phase 2: Trajectory Selection Criteria
* **Acceptance Metrics**:
  - Verify that at least one undeniably decisive passage exists within the textual noise. Accept only trajectories where exactly one isolated passage contains the strict answer-bearing field required to fully resolve the user query. The trace must point directly to that precise span and simultaneously prove why the highly confusing distractors definitively fail the test.
  - Ensure the synthetic distractors are genuinely confusable to an underlying embedding model. You must verify that the noise items exhibit immense lexical overlap with the target topic and would likely deceive a shallow vector search mechanism. Check that they strictly share the same entity family, structural formatting type, or closely adjacent temporal timeline.
  - Confirm the agent resolves lexical noise through proven contextual redundancy. Accept only if a reconstructed typographical fact is cleanly supported by the broader surrounding sentence context within the exact same noisy document. For example, if the word '2022Nobel' is corrupted, other explicit nearby terms like 'Literature' or 'Sweden' must exist to justify the agent's reconstructive leap.
  - Validate that the core failure pressure remains heavily precision-centric. Accept the tested trajectory solely if the predominant risk lies in accidentally selecting the wrong matching passage, not executing advanced external math. If the dominant cognitive burden suddenly transforms into complex multi-variable calculus, the benchmark case fundamentally strays from isolating retrieval discrimination.
* **Rejection Criteria**:
  - Reject trajectories where the designed distractor noise is demonstrably weak or transparent. If the irrelevant passages are glaringly off-topic after a swift one-line skim, the benchmark sample fundamentally fails to measure true robustness to semantic noise. Generic filler paragraphs completely unrelated to the primary entity are automatically disqualified.
  - Discard contexts where the required answer is duplicated ubiquitously across the documents. Reject the trajectory if identically valid answers can be drawn from multiple passages, eliminating any genuine need for discriminative capability. The ultimate objective is to isolate one solitary decisive chunk amidst near misses, otherwise the sample measures recall rather than precision.
  - Eliminate formats suffering from overly severe corruption lacking semantic signal. Reject any trajectory targeting a document labeled with total content loss, where the key entities are objectively unreadable disjointed symbols without surrounding context. Permitting an agent to blindly guess an answer from illegible syntactic junk actively encourages dangerous uncontrolled hallucinatory behaviors.
  - Reject cases missing uniquely discriminating metadata across the passages. If the supporting and distracting passages lack visible separating labels such as explicit dates, localized entity names, or precise headers, the case must be discarded. Without a clearly visible token-level discriminator, independent annotation of the sample becomes aggressively subjective and unstable.
