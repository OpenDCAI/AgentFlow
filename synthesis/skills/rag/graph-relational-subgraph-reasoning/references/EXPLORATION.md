# Phase 1: Environment Exploration Guide
* **Exploration Strategy**:
  - Implement Structural Subgraph Template Sampling to identify complex reasoning patterns within the corpus. The agent must scan the extracted Knowledge Graph for patterns matching Simple (single triplet), Set (one-to-many), Multi-Hop (sequential chain), or Conditional (intersection) templates. A concrete edge case is searching for a company that shares a CEO with one entity and a supplier with another to create a high-difficulty multi-hop question.
  - Apply Novel-Fact Triplet Cross-Referencing to prioritize fresh information in periodically updated streams. The agent should extract 'Subject-Relation-Object' triples from the retrieved texts and compare them against general-purpose knowledge bases like Wikidata or DBpedia. Triplets that do not exist in these global repositories are flagged as 'novel facts,' which are ideal for RAG benchmarks because they minimize the risk of the model answering from its internal training data. For example, a recent news fact about a specific product launch in 2024 is prioritized over a static historical fact about the company's founding date.
  - Execute LLM-Guided Subject Disambiguation to ensure symbolic integrity across the graph. When multiple entities share similar names, the agent must perform a multi-stage verify-and-lock procedure using metadata like 'birthplace', 'headquarters', or 'industry type'. It logs these unique identifiers as 'anchor nodes' to prevent the reasoning trajectory from drifting into homonym-based errors. A specific instance involves checking the 'Industry' tag to distinguish between 'Mercury' the chemical element and 'Mercury' the records label during a traversal.
  - Perform Intent-to-Milestone Decomposition to map high-level queries to specific triplet paths. Upon receiving a user's natural language request, the agent must decompose the goal into a sequence of required sub-nodes (milestones) that essentially form the reasoning chain. It then targets its search specifically at the relational edges that connect these milestones, ensuring the final path is mathematically sound. An edge case is breaking down the query 'how is the budget linked to the crisis' into a three-hop path involving [Budget] -> [Department] -> [Action] -> [Crisis].
* **Target Trajectory Profile**:
  - Mandate the use of Symbolic Entity-ID Ledgers to track the exact nodes visited during the traversal. The trajectory must explicitly log the unique identifiers or labels for every 'Subject', 'Predicate', and 'Object' utilized in the chain to allow for automated structural verification. If the agent moves from 'Company A' to 'CEO B', it must record the specific 'CEO of' relation edge. This record ensures that the reasoning is grounded in explicit graph structure rather than associative text mapping.
  - Demonstrate Bridge-Entity Isolation during the exploration of sequential chains. A high-quality trajectory must identify the 'bridge' node that connects two otherwise disjoint facts and log its specific contribution to the query resolution. For example, the scratchpad should note that 'FAW' serves as the necessary bridge between a sales volume and a country of origin. This demonstrates a conscious understanding of the multi-hop architecture required to solve the task.
  - Enforce Context-Necessity Auditing to ensure the agent relies strictly on the provided documents. The trajectory should show the agent actively ignoring 'easy' answers that might be sourced from its internal parametric memory in favor of the specific facts found in the bounded environment. It should document: 'Ignoring general knowledge about the capital of France; strictly using the triplet [Paris-Capital-France] found in Doc 7'. This behavior certifies that the synthesized data is a valid test of RAG capabilities rather than general latent knowledge.
  - Execute set-completeness verification for aggregation-style queries. When the goal is to collect all entities sharing a relationship (Set questions), the trajectory must list the total count of matching nodes discovered and verify that no additional edges exist for that subject in the current context. A perfect example is logging 'Found 2 projects for Ryan Otter: [Trigger, Method]; no further composition relations detected'. This ensures the final answer is exhaustive and scorable.
