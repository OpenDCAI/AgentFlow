# Phase 2: Trajectory Selection Criteria
* **Acceptance Metrics**:
  - Require a Hallucination-to-Fact Ratio of less than 0.05, meaning for every 20 true atomic events, no more than one unverified detail is permitted. You must compare your exploration notes against the raw video; if you find mentioned details like 'the car is a Ford' but the logo is illegible, the trajectory is deemed 'hallucination-prone' and rejected. This ensures the synthetic data provides a ground truth that is 100% recoverable from pixels and audio.
  - Mandate an Information Completeness Score identifying that at least 90% of the key milestones (identified via a text-LLM decomposition of the video) are captured in the trace. Perform a 'Missing Event' audit: identify any major plot-driving segments (like a person entering a room) that were skipped in the log. A sample is accepted only if the summary of the exploration notes preserves the total informational integrity of the source.
  - Verify Multi-Modal Saturation where both the audio channel (transcripts/sounds) and visual channel (actions/OCR) are represented in the trajectory milestones. You must check that the audio content contributes unique facts not found in the video and vice-versa. An accepted sample demonstrates 'Visual-only information' (e.g., red spatula) and 'Audio-only information' (e.g., narrator's specific recipe secret) integrated into a single timeline.
  - Enforce a Precise Temporal Alignment Threshold where transitions between atomic events are synchronized within a 500ms window of the actual onset/offset. You must verify that the notes for 'T=10s: Bird flies' match the exact frame where the bird becomes visible. This prevents the synthesis phase from creating a 'drifting' narrative where actions are described in the wrong order or significantly late.
* **Rejection Criteria**:
  - Immediately reject 'Generic Summary Shortcuts' where the description relies on common-sense priors or title-leaks rather than custom video events. You should simulate an 'image-only' or 'text-only' test; if the description can be generated by looking at the first frame and the title (e.g., 'How to make pasta'), the sample is rejected. We demand 'Deep Video Perception' where the specific, unique movements of the chef are the only way to answer.
  - Discard any trajectory with Unverifiable Background Guesses where the model describes low-resolution peripheral details that cannot be human-verified. For example, if the log says 'the background person is smiling' but the face is only 5 pixels wide, discard the sample. This protects the benchmark from training on 'lucky guesses' and encourages the model to say 'unclear' when resolution is insufficient.
  - Eliminate 'Modality Contradiction Samples' where the audio transcript explicitly refutes the visual evidence without the agent acknowledging the conflict. For instance, if a narrator says 'It is a sunny day' but the video shows pouring rain, and the notes simply repeat the 'sunny day' claim, the sample is rejected. This enforces a 'Strict Fidelity' policy where the agent must report what is physically seen over what is verbally claimed.
