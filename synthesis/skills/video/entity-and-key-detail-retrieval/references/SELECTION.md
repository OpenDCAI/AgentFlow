# Phase 2: Trajectory Selection Criteria
* **Acceptance Metrics**:
  - Mandate exactly one 'Object-to-Distractor Disambiguation Milestone' where the agent successfully separates the target from identical-looking neighbors in a crowded field. You must verify whether the agent can independently track the 'red cube' when it is abruptly placed next to multiple structurally identical cubes of varying colors. An accepted sample demonstrates that the 'Detail Retrieval' relies squarely on the target's unique attribute (color) and does not collapse into a broad 'general block' assumption.
  - Require 'Boundary Intersection Precision' where the spatial and temporal contact between two distinct entities is localized within a rigid 3-frame window. You should deploy a proximity sweep to ensure the agent captures the exact micro-second of physical engagement between the end-effector and the object. Accept the sample only if the 'Interaction-Start' frame is isolated as an undeniable spatial threshold defining when the two distinct entity slots merge into one kinetic unit.
  - Enforce 'Invariance Threshold Verification' to ensure the retrieved entity properties are completely stable across aggressive background randomization or lighting shifts. First, compare the entity's visual representation on a bright stark background versus a dark or cluttered background, evaluating if the tracking holds. Accept the trajectory only if the 'Object Identity' persists across the shift, protecting the benchmark from agents that overfit to specific scene arrangements.
  - Mandate rigorous 'Adversarial Absence Grounding' to formally confirm that identified missing items exist genuinely beyond spatial rendering boundaries, guaranteeing zero-hallucination protocols. You must implement independent pixel sweeps guaranteeing the omitted item lacks even fractional pixel reflections that might trigger false positive generative recognition. Accepting a definitive 'not located' verification holds the model accountable for establishing proven visual omissions rather than assuming standard habits.
  - Require 'Anchor-Fact Verifiability' ensuring that any text, number, or symbol extracted for downstream mathematical computation or web search is distinctly legible in at least one keyframe. You must perform a zoomed manual inspection of the target crop zone to confirm the bounding box contains rigidly readable pixels rather than hallucinated compression blur. Accept the sample only if the OCR confidence or fine-grained texture allows a standard human reviewer to reach the exact same text or numeric conclusion independently.
* **Rejection Criteria**:
  - Immediately reject 'Holistic Overfitting Shortcuts' where the retrieval answer is easily recoverable by looking at the global background or scene gist rather than parsing the specific entity. You should systematically purge samples where common-sense priors natively bypass the need for fine-grained object-centric visual retrieval. The validity of the deduction must hinge exclusively on distinguishing the unique 'slots' in the video rather than concluding 'the robot is in a kitchen, therefore it probably grabbed a cup'.
  - Discard any trajectory exhibiting 'Entity Morphing or Identity Drift' where the target object's identity is lost, duplicated, or swapped with a nearby distractor during a fast kinetic movement. First, audit the timeline evaluating the 'Entity Lock'; block the data if the 'Red Cube' suddenly starts being tracked as a 'Yellow Cube' after a collision. This critical rejection safeguard prevents the synthetic data from validating architectural failures in long-term object permanence.
  - Eliminate 'Disjointed Interaction Samples' where the primary agent and the target object are never displayed simultaneously in the same logical spatial context. First, check the frame logic for 'Causal Connection'; if the robot 'pushes' the cube but the camera view of the cube is wholly occluded or presented from a disjointed abstract perspective, the physical outcome is unresolvable. We must exclusively retain 'Spatially Contiguous' interactions where the entities share a provable geometric plane.
  - Immediately reject 'Category-Bias Retrieval Shortcuts' that completely bypass rigorous visual tracking dependency by leveraging static external common-sense distributions automatically. You must exclude ungrounded prompts that are universally solvable by deducing generic placement priors like 'milk is usually stored inside the typical refrigerator'. Valid detailed retrieval establishes exact object presence independently of overriding social or environmental stereotypes.
