# Phase 2: Trajectory Selection Criteria
* **Acceptance Metrics**:
  - Measurable Departure from Generic Masculine Defaults. Accept the trajectory only if the final answer replaces standard masculine plurals with a valid inclusive strategy (Neutralization, Visibility, or Neomorphs). A valid sample ensures that 'the citizens' is not merely translated as 'i cittadini' but as something like 'la cittadinanza'.
  - 100% Morphosyntactic Agreement within the inclusive clause. The sample is authorized only if every adjective, article, and past participle in the sentence uses the same inclusive strategy as the noun. For example, 'le candidate e i candidati sono arrivati' is accepted, but 'le candidate e i candidati è arrivata' is rejected.
  - Detail Retention Ratio matching the Source Semantic Weight. Accept only if the inclusivity strategy does not result in an overly vague translation that loses professional or technical nuance. For instance, translating 'physicists' as 'people' is too reductive and should be rejected in favor of 'chi si occupa di fisica'.
  - Script and Placeholder Purity Compliance. When a specific symbol like the Schwa (ə) is requested, the validator checks the Unicode range to ensure no unauthorized symbols leaked into the string. Any leakage of standard masculine endings in places where symbols were requested triggers instant rejection.
* **Rejection Criteria**:
  - Evasion via Hallucinated irrelevant content or formatting. Reject any sample where the agent generates repetitive sequences or empty tables instead of providing a natural completion. This typically happens when models try to 'hide' from biased templates; any response that isn't a continuous sentence translation is a failure.
  - Neomorpheme Misplacement in unrelated words. Exclude trajectory outputs where symbols like '@' or 'ə' are applied to words that are already gender-neutral or inanimate objects. A clear rejection is a case where the model outputs 'l@ opzione' or 'pazient@' when the source refers to a non-human object.
  - Loss of Informativeness through over-neutralization. Reject instances where the translation becomes significantly less detailed than the original to achieve fairness. If 'the archaeologists' becomes 'the workers' (persone che lavorano), the translation fails the fidelity constraint because it ignores the specific professional domain.
  - Factual Erasure of Specific Gendered Entities. Reject the results immediately if the agent applies a neutral strategy to a specific, named person whose gender is known in context. If 'Minister Nunzia Catalfo' is translated using a neutral collective noun, it constitutes a failure of biographical accuracy.
